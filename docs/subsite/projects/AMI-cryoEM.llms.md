## The research question

Amyloid fibrils can adopt different structures, and those differences matter for their biological effects. AMI-CryoML investigates how small aggregation modulators affect both aggregation kinetics and the structures that form. Linking experiments with structural data provides the basis for learning relationships that can be tested in predictive models.

## What the project sets out to do

01 / RESEARCH AIM

### Map the modulators

Collect aggregation modulators and characterise their chemical and mechanistic properties.

02 / RESEARCH AIM

### Measure and resolve

Use thioflavin-T assays to measure kinetic effects across prions, CsgA and α-synuclein, and cryo-EM to resolve fibril polymorphs.

03 / RESEARCH AIM

### Connect data with prediction

Extend AmyloGraph with modulator–kinetics–structure records and train models to predict modulator effects.
