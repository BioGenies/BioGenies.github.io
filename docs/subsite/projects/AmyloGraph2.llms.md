## The research question

AmyloGraph began as a resource for amyloid–amyloid interactions. This project extends its scope to a wider range of self-assembly modulators and the experimental context in which they act. The resulting curated evidence supports models that go beyond predicting whether a protein aggregates, towards understanding how its aggregation can be changed.

## What the project sets out to do

01 / RESEARCH AIM

### Expand the evidence base

Extend AmyloGraph to non-protein modulators, including experimental conditions and other essential metadata.

02 / RESEARCH AIM

### Predict modulation

Train machine learning models to predict how a modulator affects amyloid aggregation.

03 / RESEARCH AIM

### Explore new modulators

Develop generative models and assess proposed modulators computationally and experimentally.
