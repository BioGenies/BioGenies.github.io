## The research question

Microbial proteins may influence the aggregation of human amyloid-forming proteins. LIMAD focuses on the identification of microbial candidates and their possible cross-interactions with Aβ42 and α-synuclein, combining evidence review, sequence screening and computational investigation.

## What the project sets out to do

01 / RESEARCH AIM

### Find candidate proteins

Review microbes associated with Alzheimer’s and Parkinson’s diseases and screen their proteins for amyloid potential.

02 / RESEARCH AIM

### Investigate cross-interactions

Combine machine learning and structural biology to investigate interactions with Aβ42 and α-synuclein.

03 / RESEARCH AIM

### Develop testable hypotheses

Identify microbial contributions to aggregation and prioritise candidates for future studies of possible targets and prevention strategies.
