# Conference and society marks

These marks identify the recorded events and their organising communities. Their respective organisations retain ownership of their marks. Original images are stored unchanged; the ISMB 2024 logo at the left of its official banner is displayed with CSS cropping.

The machine-readable sources, official links and matching rules are in `data/conference-logos.json` at the project root. Year-specific entries apply only to their recorded edition. Society, organiser, series and network marks are labelled as such and may identify multiple editions.
