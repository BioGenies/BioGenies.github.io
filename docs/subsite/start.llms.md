# Find your way into BioGenies

A starting point for students, research collaborators and users of BioGenies software and databases.

Whether you have a question, an idea for a collaboration or a dataset to analyse, here is a path into our work.

## For students

EXPLORE · LEARN · GET IN TOUCH

### Find a question that interests you.

We work where biology, data analysis and programming meet. You can start with the science without already knowing every method.

1.  **Explore the questions.** Read our [research stories in plain language](../subsite/stories.llms.md), then explore [research topics](../subsite/research.llms.md#our-research-topics).
2.  **Meet the people and their work.** Browse the [team](../subsite/team.llms.md) and [completed theses and dissertations](../subsite/theses.llms.md) for examples of research directions.
3.  **Start a conversation.** Tell us about your interests, experience and the kind of project you would like to explore. [Email BioGenies](mailto:biogenies@umb.edu.pl?subject=Student%20research%20enquiry) to ask about possibilities.

[Explore the imputomics story →](../subsite/stories.llms.md#imputomics)

## For researchers & collaborators

SHARED QUESTIONS · COMPLEMENTARY EXPERTISE

### Begin with the question you want to answer.

Our research connects protein aggregation, multiomics, statistical methods and machine learning. Existing projects and publications are a useful starting point for finding common ground.

1.  **Find a shared interest.** Explore our [research topics](../subsite/research.llms.md#our-research-topics) and [project portfolio](../index_projects.llms.md).
2.  **Find the relevant expertise.** Meet the [team](../subsite/team.llms.md) and [collaborators](../subsite/colab.llms.md), and follow their publication links.
3.  **Bring an idea.** [Email the group](mailto:biogenies@umb.edu.pl?subject=Research%20collaboration) with a short description of your question, available data or experiments, and the expertise you would like to connect.

[Browse our publications →](../subsite/publications.llms.md)

## For software & database users

EXPLORE · UNDERSTAND · APPLY

### Find a resource for your next analysis.

Our catalogue includes web applications, R packages and curated biological databases. Each resource has its own scope and documentation.

1.  **Find a resource.** Browse [software and databases](../subsite/software.llms.md) for links to applications and source code.
2.  **Understand the research behind it.** Read the [imputomics](../subsite/stories.llms.md#imputomics), [AmyloGraph](../subsite/stories.llms.md#amylograph) and [HaDeX2](../subsite/stories.llms.md#hadex2) stories.
3.  **Try an idea before a full analysis.** Use the [imputomics story](../subsite/stories.llms.md#imputomics) to explore why estimates depend on the method and the missing values.

[Open the software catalogue →](../subsite/software.llms.md)
